## Mouse
Select items and buttons on screen. Open officer and equipment details from their respective entries, and follow the guidance shown in each screen.

## Keyboard
Basic navigation uses the arrow keys or WASD. Enter or Space confirms, and Esc cancels. Check each screen's hints for context-specific commands.

## Gamepad
Navigate with the directional pad or left stick, confirm with the primary confirm button, and go back with the primary cancel button. Button labels vary between controllers; follow the in-game hints and configuration.

## Platform plans
PC support covers Windows and macOS. Android and iOS are both planned after the PC launch. NS remains a future plan. Release dates and system requirements will be announced when confirmed.

This is a basic controls draft based on the current input configuration. It does not yet document every screen-specific shortcut.
