## Check both officers
Before choosing a command, inspect both officers' HP, the spirit gauge and available actions. Strength alone does not determine the result; consider current condition and equipment too.

![A duel in the rain, from a development recording](/assets/screens/duel.webp)

## Read command descriptions
Use actions in response to the situation. When trying an unfamiliar command, read its explanation and observe what changes after the exchange. Do not assume a single option guarantees victory in every duel.

## Separate attributes from combat effects
Equipment can affect displayed attributes, while some effects are applied only during combat. The website's base data should not be treated as a direct measure of final duel damage.

## About the footage
Rain, snow and night scenes on this site come from Chinese-language development recordings. They may include test controls and older values. They demonstrate the game's actual visuals; current rules should be checked in the game.
