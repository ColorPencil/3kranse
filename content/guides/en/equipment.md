## Open the equipment details
Inspect the officer's equipment slots and item tooltips. Read the name, attack, defense, weight and requirements. A famous name or a high single stat is not enough to decide whether an item suits its wielder.

## Compare weight and capability
Weight is part of equipment selection. Check the current officer state and requirements in the game. The website catalogue extracts basic attack, defense and weight, without revealing acquisition locations or hidden conditions.

## Distinguish innate traits from talents
Read innate traits separately from the talent tree. Exclusive stratagems and combat techniques may require learning through the relevant talent. Seeing an ability's name in a catalogue does not mean it is available at the beginning of a campaign.

## What the public catalogue includes
The officer selection requires a base strength, intelligence or prestige of at least 90. Only selected equipment is shown. Items above level 5 appear by name alone. Acquisition methods, hidden conditions and unrevealed effects remain for you to discover in the game.
