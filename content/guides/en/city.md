## Read the city information
A city supports both administration and military preparation. Look at gold, food, agriculture, industry, population and rule. Before improving a single value, ask what your faction currently needs most.

## Give people a purpose
Officers have several attributes and different innate traits. Before sending every famous general to the front, consider who will remain to manage the city. Read an action's explanation and compare the city before and after carrying it out.

## Connect administration to your campaign
Make a habit of checking supplies and troop strength before marching. Consider the city's condition after the army leaves as well. Study neighbouring cities and factions, then distinguish your long-term ambition from what you can accomplish this turn.

## A beginner's checklist
- What is the state of the city's gold and food?
- What roles suit the officers available to you?
- Is it time to fight, or time to prepare?
