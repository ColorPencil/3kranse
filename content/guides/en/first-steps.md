## Get to know your faction
After choosing a ruler and faction, open the city information and officer list. Check your gold, food, city conditions and available people before planning your first move. Starting positions vary by scenario; a recorded campaign is an example rather than a universal opening.

## Look beyond strength
An officer's intelligence, prestige, command and innate trait also matter. Read the officer and equipment details before assigning a role. The website catalogue lists base attributes, without equipment bonuses or temporary combat effects. Those numbers alone do not predict a battle's outcome.

## Start with a small goal
1. Check the city's gold and food.
2. Open an officer's details and read their attributes, trait and equipment.
3. Review troop types and formation before marching.
4. On the battlefield, inspect movement range and terrain before issuing orders.
5. Review the result before planning the next turn.

You do not need to master every system at once. Begin with one city and one army, then develop your own approach.
