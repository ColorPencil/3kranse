## Current focus
Development and stability on PC come first. PC support covers Windows and macOS, with mouse, keyboard and gamepad controls. The Steam store page is in preparation; the official site will link to it once it is public.

## Mobile follows the PC launch
An Android test build exists. Both Android and iOS are planned, but mobile is not part of the PC launch. Screen layout and controls need further adaptation before a mobile release.

## Future plans
NS is a future platform goal. This is not an announcement of a release date, certification status or distribution arrangement. Dates and system requirements will be published when confirmed.

## Building the official site
The website prioritizes Japanese, Chinese and English. Beginner guides are editorial drafts awaiting review and will be maintained as the game changes. The Korean version is not yet being produced.
